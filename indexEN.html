FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before commissioning your life-size hero.

How does shipping and delivery work for the figures?

The management and cost of transport are the buyer's responsibility, but we take care of guaranteeing maximum security professional packaging. We prepare your custom piece in a reinforced wooden crate, with foam and cork, fully protected for the trip. Once ready, you can come pick it up in person at our workshop in Málaga, or send the courier company of your choice to pick it up at our facilities.

How long does it take to manufacture a large-scale figure?

Since it is a 100% handcrafted job, each figure is unique and there is no standard manufacturing time. The exact timeframe depends on the size, the geometric complexity of the model, the level of post-processing (puttying, sanding, and hand-painting), and the workshop's current workload. In projects of this magnitude, unexpected technical challenges can arise, so we evaluate each case individually and give you a fully personalized time estimate.

Can I request a character that is not in the catalog?

Of course! If you have a character in mind (from anime, video games, or movies) that is not on the website, write to us on WhatsApp. If the 3D model exists with enough quality, we can print it at the size you want.

What maintenance does the paint on the figures need?

Our figures are protected with high-durability varnishes (matte or satin depending on the finish). You only need to use a duster or a dry microfiber cloth for dust. Avoid using abrasive chemicals or exposing them to direct and extreme sunlight for many hours a day.
